( About the Gym ) 01 — 05

A SPACE TO MOVE BETTER, FEEL STRONGER, LIVE HEALTHIER.

Alphonsians' Gym is a student-focused fitness space at Alphonsa College. Students can use the gym freely during available hours and access equipment based on current availability.

Beyond physical training, the platform supports students with workout guidance, wellness resources and practical fitness tools.
